Energy analytics

greenpulse-energy-monitoring

An AI-powered energy monitoring and optimization platform for commercial buildings. It combines real-time consumption dashboards, ML anomaly detection, LSTM demand forecasting and carbon tracking.

Drawing

Highlights

  • LSTM models forecast energy usage up to 7 days ahead, with hourly load forecasts and confidence intervals.
  • Anomaly detection flags equipment malfunctions, after-hours consumption and demand spikes.
  • Cost analysis covers time-of-use rate and demand-charge optimization.
